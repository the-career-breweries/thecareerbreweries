# THE 7% RULE

Studies suggest that in emotional situations, communication is:
*   **7%** Words
*   **38%** Tone of Voice
*   **55%** Body Language

You can memorize the perfect apology, but if your arms are crossed and your eyes are darting, **they will not believe you.**

# PROXEMICS: THE SPACE BETWEEN US

In a crowded terminal or an aircraft cabin, space is a weapon and a shield.

*   **Intimate Space (0-1.5 ft):** For secrets and emergencies. (Invading this triggers a threat response in passengers).
*   **Personal Space (1.5-4 ft):** The standard "customer service" zone.
*   **Social Space (4-12 ft):** Addressing a group or making announcements.

**Pro Tip:** If a passenger is aggressive, slightly angle your body (45 degrees). Standing perfectly square is subconsciously interpreted as a challenge to fight.

# THE S.O.F.T.E.N. TECHNIQUE

Master your physical presence:

*   **S**mile (When appropriate, not during a tragedy)
*   **O**pen posture (No crossed arms, no hiding hands behind your back)
*   **F**orward lean (Shows you are engaged and listening)
*   **T**ouch (A firm handshake to build instant trust)
*   **E**ye Contact (Steady, but break it naturally every few seconds)
*   **N**od (Validates that you hear them)

# THE SILENT INTERVIEW (ACTIVITY)

**The Challenge:**
1. Form pairs. One person is the interviewer, one is the candidate.
2. The interviewer will ask 3 questions.
3. The candidate must answer... **without speaking.** 
4. You may only use posture, micro-expressions, and gestures.

*Can you project confidence in total silence?*

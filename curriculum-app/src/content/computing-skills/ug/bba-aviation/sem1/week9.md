# Session 9
## Computing Skills

---

## Key Concepts
- **Data Visualization**: Representing numerical data graphically using charts.
- **Chart Types**: Choosing the appropriate chart (Pie, Line, Column) based on the data type.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Chart**: A graphical representation of data.
- **Legend**: A key that explains the colors, symbols, or patterns used in a chart.


---

## Mnemonic: PLC
- P - Pie (Parts of a whole)
- L - Line (Trends over time)
- C - Column (Comparisons)


---

## Knowledge Check (BTL)
- **Remember**: Identify the purpose of a Pie chart.
- **Understand**: Explain when a Line chart is more appropriate than a Column chart.
- **Apply**: Visualize a monthly sales dataset with a Column chart.
- **Analyze**: Interpret a provided chart to find the highest performing month.
- **Evaluate**: Critique a chart that uses misleading scales.
- **Create**: Design a dashboard with three different chart types.

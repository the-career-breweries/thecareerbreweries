# 5.2.1 Introduction to email clients (Outlook, Gmail)

* **Email Clients:** Desktop applications like Microsoft Outlook or Apple Mail.
* **Webmail:** Accessing email via a browser (Gmail, Outlook.com).
* **Professionalism:** An email address should be professional (e.g., `firstname.lastname@airline.com`), not casual (`flyboy99@yahoo.com`).

---

# 5.2.2 Composing, replying, and forwarding emails

* **Subject Line:** Must be clear, specific, and actionable (e.g., 'Action Required: Updated Roster for March').
* **Salutation & Sign-off:** Always use professional greetings ('Dear...', 'Hello...') and closings ('Best regards,', 'Sincerely,').
* **Reply vs Reply All:** ONLY use Reply All if every single person on the chain needs to know your specific response.

---

# 5.2.3 Using CC and BCC appropriately

* **To:** The primary recipient(s) who are expected to take action.
* **CC (Carbon Copy):** Kept in the loop for informational purposes, but no action is expected of them.
* **BCC (Blind Carbon Copy):** Recipients are hidden from everyone else. 
  * *Use Case:* Mass emailing 50 clients to protect their privacy, or secretly keeping a manager informed.

---

# 5.2.4 Attaching files and understanding size limits

* **Attachments:** Clicking the paperclip icon to send files (PDFs, Spreadsheets).
* **Size Limits:** Most email servers reject emails larger than 20MB - 25MB.
* **The Solution:** For large files (like high-res videos or databases), upload the file to Cloud Storage (Drive/OneDrive) and email a *link* to the file instead.

---

# 5.2.5 Organizing inbox using folders, labels, and filters

* **Folders/Labels:** Creating specific buckets for emails (e.g., 'HR Updates', 'Client A', 'Travel Receipts').
* **Filters/Rules:** Automating your inbox. 
  * *Example:* Set a rule: "If email is from the CEO, flag as High Importance and move to 'Urgent' folder."
* **Archiving vs Deleting:** Archiving removes it from the inbox but keeps it searchable forever. Deleting moves it to Trash to be permanently erased.

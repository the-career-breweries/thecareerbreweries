# 2.2.1 Paragraph alignment and justification

* **Location:** Home Tab -> Paragraph Group.
* **Left Align (`Ctrl + L`):** Default standard for English text.
* **Center Align (`Ctrl + E`):** Used for titles and headings.
* **Right Align (`Ctrl + R`):** Often used for dates in formal letters.
* **Justify (`Ctrl + J`):** Aligns text to both left and right margins, creating clean edges (common in newspapers and textbooks).

---

# 2.2.2 Line spacing and paragraph spacing

* **Line Spacing:** The vertical space between lines of text within the same paragraph.
  * *Standard:* 1.15 or 1.5 for readability. 2.0 (Double) for academic drafts.
* **Paragraph Spacing:** The space Before and After a paragraph.
  * *Best Practice:* Add space *after* paragraphs instead of pressing 'Enter' twice. Adjust via Layout Tab -> Spacing.

---

# 2.2.3 Creating bulleted and numbered lists

* **Bulleted Lists:** Used for unordered items (e.g., a packing list, features).
  * *Creation:* Type `*` and press Space, or click the Bullet icon in the Home tab.
* **Numbered Lists:** Used for sequential steps or ranked items (e.g., emergency procedures).
  * *Creation:* Type `1.` and press Space, or click the Numbering icon.
* **Adjusting:** Use 'Increase/Decrease Indent' buttons to move list items left or right.

---

# 2.2.4 Multilevel lists for complex documents

* **Definition:** Lists that contain sub-lists (e.g., 1, 1.1, 1.1.1 or I, A, 1, a).
* **How to Create:**
  1. Start a standard numbered list.
  2. Press `Enter` to go to the next line.
  3. Press `Tab` to indent and create a sub-level.
  4. Press `Shift + Tab` to promote the item back to the higher level.
* **Usage:** Essential for technical manuals, legal contracts, and structured reports.

---

# 2.2.5 Indentations and tab stops

* **First Line Indent:** Indents only the first line of a paragraph. (Common in books). Usually created by pressing `Tab` once.
* **Hanging Indent:** The first line is flush left, but subsequent lines are indented. (Used in Bibliographies/References).
* **Tab Stops:** Define exactly where the cursor jumps when you press `Tab`. Set them by clicking on the horizontal ruler at the top of the page.

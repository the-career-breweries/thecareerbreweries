# 4.3.1 Inserting and formatting images and icons

* **Inserting:** Insert Tab -> Pictures / Icons.
* **Resizing:** Always drag from the *corner* handles to keep the image proportional. Dragging the sides will stretch and distort the image.
* **Alignment:** Use the red 'Smart Guides' that appear as you drag an image to align it perfectly centered or flush with text.

---

# 4.3.2 Drawing and styling shapes and text boxes

* **Inserting Shapes:** Insert -> Shapes. Hold the `Shift` key while drawing a circle or square to make it a perfect circle/square.
* **Text in Shapes:** Simply click a shape and start typing; the text is automatically centered inside.
* **Formatting:** Shape Format Tab. Adjust Shape Fill, Shape Outline, and Shape Effects (shadows, reflections).

---

# 4.3.3 Using SmartArt for process flows and hierarchies

* **What is SmartArt?** A tool that instantly converts boring bullet points into professional graphics.
* **How to use:** Highlight your bullet points -> Right-click -> 'Convert to SmartArt'.
* **Types:** 
  * *List/Process:* Good for timelines or steps.
  * *Hierarchy:* Good for organizational charts.
  * *Cycle:* Good for continuous loops (e.g., feedback loops).

---

# 4.3.4 Inserting tables and charts into slides

* **Inserting Tables:** Insert -> Table. Works identically to MS Word.
* **Inserting Charts:** Insert -> Chart. This opens a mini-Excel window where you paste your data. The chart then renders in PowerPoint.
* **Best Practice:** Simplify data on slides. Do not paste a massive 50-row Excel spreadsheet. Extract the key numbers and make a clean, simple chart.

---

# 4.3.5 Embedding video and audio clips

* **Inserting Video:** Insert -> Video. You can upload from your PC or link from YouTube.
* **Playback Options:** Video Format/Playback Tab. Set it to start 'Automatically' or 'When Clicked'. 
* **Trimming:** You can trim the start/end of an embedded video directly inside PowerPoint without external video editors.

---

# 4.3.6 Hands-on: Creating a visual safety briefing

* **Step 1:** Create a new slide titled 'Evacuation Procedure'.
* **Step 2:** Type 3 steps: Brace, Unbuckle, Evacuate.
* **Step 3:** Convert the text into a 'Process' SmartArt graphic.
* **Step 4:** Insert an icon of an airplane next to the title.
* **Step 5:** Insert an emergency exit graphic and apply a 'Drop Shadow' shape effect.

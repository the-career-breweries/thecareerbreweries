## Key Concepts
- **Text Formatting**: Applying styles like bold, italics, underline, and changing fonts and colors to emphasize text.
- **Paragraph Formatting**: Adjusting alignment, line spacing, and indentation for readability.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Alignment**: The positioning of text relative to the margins (left, center, right, justified).
- **Line Spacing**: The vertical distance between lines of text in a paragraph.


---

## Mnemonic: FAS
- F - Font
- A - Alignment
- S - Spacing

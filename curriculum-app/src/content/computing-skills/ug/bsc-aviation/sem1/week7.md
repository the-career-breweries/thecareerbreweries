## Key Concepts
- **Spreadsheet Basics**: Understanding the grid structure of rows, columns, and cells.
- **Basic Operators & Formulas**: Using mathematical operators (+, -, *, /) to perform calculations.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Cell**: The intersection of a row and a column in a spreadsheet.
- **Formula**: An expression that operates on values in a range of cells.


---

## Mnemonic: BODMAS
- B - Brackets
- O - Order
- D - Division
- M - Multiplication
- A - Addition
- S - Subtraction (Excel follows this)

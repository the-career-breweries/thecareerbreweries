# 5.5.1 Introduction to video conferencing (Zoom, Teams)

* **The Shift:** Remote work and global aviation teams rely heavily on Video Conferencing.
* **Zoom / MS Teams:** Industry leaders for video calls, webinars, and enterprise communication.
* **Key Features:** Waiting rooms, host controls (muting participants), chat boxes, and recording capabilities.

---

# 5.5.2 Setting up and scheduling a virtual meeting

* **Scheduling:** Generating a calendar invite with a unique meeting link, meeting ID, and passcode.
* **Hardware Check:** Always test your microphone and webcam in the settings *before* joining the call.
* **Backgrounds:** Use a clean, distraction-free physical background, or utilize 'Virtual Backgrounds'/'Background Blur' for professionalism.

---

# 5.5.3 Screen sharing and meeting etiquette

* **Screen Sharing:** Sharing your entire desktop vs sharing a specific application window (Application sharing is safer to prevent accidental pop-ups).
* **Mute Discipline:** Keep your microphone muted when you are not speaking to eliminate background noise.
* **Eye Contact:** Look at the *camera* lens when speaking, not at the screen, to simulate real eye contact.

---

# 5.5.4 Professional instant messaging (Slack, Teams chat)

* **Slack / Teams Chat:** Replacing internal email with real-time text channels.
* **Channels vs DMs:** Use specific channels for team topics (e.g., `#flight-ops`), and Direct Messages for 1-on-1 chats.
* **Etiquette:** 
  * Use threads to keep conversations organized.
  * Set your status (e.g., 'In a Meeting', 'Out of Office').
  * Keep messages concise and professional.

## Key Concepts
- **Inserting Tables**: Organizing data into rows and columns within a Word document.
- **Report Structure**: Using headers, footers, page numbers, and a table of contents.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Header/Footer**: Areas in the top and bottom margins of a page where you can insert text or graphics.
- **Table of Contents**: An organized list of the parts of a book or document.


---

## Mnemonic: THP
- T - Table
- H - Header
- P - Page Number


---

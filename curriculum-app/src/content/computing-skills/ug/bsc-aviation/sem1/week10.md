# Session 10
## Computing Skills

---

## Key Concepts
- **Sorting & Filtering**: Organizing and narrowing down large datasets to find specific information.
- **Conditional Formatting**: Applying specific formatting to cells that meet certain criteria to highlight trends.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Sorting**: Arranging data in a specified order (alphabetical, numerical, chronological).
- **Filtering**: Hiding rows of data that do not meet specified criteria.


---

## Mnemonic: SFC
- S - Sort
- F - Filter
- C - Condition


---

## Knowledge Check (BTL)
- **Remember**: Define Conditional Formatting.
- **Understand**: Explain how filtering differs from deleting data.
- **Apply**: Filter a dataset to show only sales above $1000.
- **Analyze**: Analyze the impact of applying multiple filters to a large table.
- **Evaluate**: Determine the best conditional formatting rule to highlight failing grades.
- **Create**: Construct a dynamic inventory sheet that highlights low stock automatically.

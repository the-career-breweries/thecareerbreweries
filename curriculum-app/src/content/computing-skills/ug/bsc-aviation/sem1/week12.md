## Key Concepts
- **Themes and Master Slide**: Applying consistent branding, fonts, and colors across all slides automatically.
- **Professional Consistency**: Ensuring headers, logos, and footers remain strictly aligned throughout the deck.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Theme**: A predefined set of colors, fonts, and visual effects that you apply to your slides.
- **Slide Master**: The top slide in a hierarchy that stores information about the theme and slide layouts.


---

## Mnemonic: TMC
- T - Theme
- M - Master
- C - Consistency

- **Remember**: What is the Slide Master?

- **Analyze**: Compare a presentation with a cohesive theme vs one without.

# 🧠 Key Concept

- Theme vs Template vs Slide Master Explained

- Three terms that are easy to confuse:

- A useful way to remember the difference: a Theme is what a deck looks like, a Template is a ready-made starting deck, and the Slide Master is where those looks are actually controlled from behind the scenes.

---

# 🧠 Key Concept

- Using the Slide Master for Consistent Branding

- Editing the Slide Master once updates every slide in the presentation that uses it — far faster and more consistent than editing each slide individually.

- Common branding elements added via the Slide Master:

- Opened from View tab  Slide Master, changes made there — such as a new font or footer — automatically appear on every normal slide once back in the regular editing view.

- Quick Tips
- Themes keep colours and fonts consistent across all slides
- **Slide Master edits all slides at once** — change once, apply everywhere
- **Don't mix too many fonts or colours** — ONE theme per presentation
- Templates = pre-built layouts; Themes = colours + fonts + effects
- Professional tip: Logo on Slide Master appears on every slide

## Key Concepts
- **Document Creation**: The fundamentals of opening, typing, editing, and saving a document in Microsoft Word.
- **Word UI Elements**: Navigating the Ribbon, Quick Access Toolbar, and Status Bar.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Ribbon**: The strip of buttons and icons located above the work area in MS Office applications.
- **Cursor**: The flashing vertical line that indicates where text will be inserted.


---

## Mnemonic: TOS
- T - Type
- O - Organize
- S - Save

- **Remember**: Identify the Ribbon in MS Word.

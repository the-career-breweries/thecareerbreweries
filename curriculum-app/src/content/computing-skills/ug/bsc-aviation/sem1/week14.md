## Computing Skills

## Key Concepts
- **Cyber Safety**: Protecting digital identity, recognizing phishing attempts, and maintaining strong passwords.
- **Digital Etiquette**: Professionalism and appropriate behavior when communicating online.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Phishing**: The fraudulent practice of sending emails purporting to be from reputable companies to induce individuals to reveal personal info.
- **Malware**: Software that is specifically designed to disrupt, damage, or gain unauthorized access to a computer system.


---

## Mnemonic: STOP
- S - Stop

- O - Observe
- P - Proceed safely

## Knowledge Check (BTL)
- **Remember**: Define malware.
- **Understand**: Explain how a strong password differs from a weak one.
- **Apply**: Identify the signs of a phishing attempt in a provided sample email.
- **Analyze**: Deconstruct a URL to determine if a website is secure (HTTPS vs HTTP).
- **Evaluate**: Assess the risks of using public Wi-Fi without a VPN.
- **Create**: Develop a set of cyber safety rules for a small office.

## Computing Skills

## Key Concepts
- **Internet Navigation**: Using web browsers effectively, utilizing bookmarks, and understanding URLs.
- **Cloud Storage & Email**: Storing files online (Google Drive, OneDrive) and basic professional email usage.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Cloud Storage**: A model of computer data storage in which digital data is stored in logical pools on multiple servers.
- **Bookmark**: A saved shortcut that directs your browser to a specific webpage.


---

## Mnemonic: WEB
- W - World Wide Web
- E - Email
- B - Browser

## Knowledge Check (BTL)
- **Remember**: Name two popular cloud storage providers.
- **Understand**: Explain the difference between CC and BCC in an email.
- **Apply**: Navigate to a specific aviation authority website and bookmark it.
- **Analyze**: Analyze the advantages of cloud storage over a USB drive.
- **Evaluate**: Determine the most professional way to attach a large file to an email.
- **Create**: Compose a professional email sharing a link to a cloud-stored document.

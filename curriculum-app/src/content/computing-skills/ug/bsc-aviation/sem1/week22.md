# 4.2.1 Applying and modifying built-in design themes

* **Design Tab:** Contains professionally crafted Themes (color palettes, fonts, and background graphics).
* **Applying a Theme:** Click a theme to apply it to all slides. 
* **Variants:** Next to the themes, you can click 'Variants' to keep the design but change the color scheme (e.g., from Blue to Green).

---

# 4.2.2 Customizing theme colors and fonts

* **Customizing Colors:** Design Tab -> Variants -> Colors. You can set specific RGB codes to match your airline/corporate branding.
* **Customizing Fonts:** Design Tab -> Variants -> Fonts. Sets the master Heading and Body fonts for the entire presentation.
* **Consistency:** Always use these global settings rather than changing font colors manually on every single slide.

---

# 4.2.3 Formatting slide backgrounds

* **Background Styles:** Design Tab -> Format Background.
* **Solid/Gradient Fill:** Change the slide canvas to a specific color or gradient.
* **Picture Fill:** Use a company photo as the background. 
  * *Crucial Step:* Increase the 'Transparency' slider so the background image doesn't make the text unreadable!

---

# 4.2.4 Working with the Slide Master for global changes

* **What is the Slide Master?** The behind-the-scenes architectural blueprint of your slides (View Tab -> Slide Master).
* **Why use it?** If you want to add a company logo to the bottom right of *every* slide, do not paste it 50 times. Paste it *once* on the Master Slide, and it appears everywhere.
* **Exiting:** You must click 'Close Master View' to return to normal editing.

---

# 4.2.5 Creating a custom corporate template

* **Creating a Template (`.potx`):** Once you have set up your master slides, colors, and logos, go to File -> Save As.
* **Change File Type:** Select 'PowerPoint Template (*.potx)'.
* **Benefit:** Future presentations can be started from this template, ensuring every employee creates slides that are 100% on-brand.

---

# 4.2.6 Hands-on: Applying consistent branding to slides

* **Step 1:** Open the 5-slide company intro.
* **Step 2:** Go to View -> Slide Master.
* **Step 3:** Insert a small 'Company Logo' shape in the top right corner of the primary master slide.
* **Step 4:** Change the Master Title font to a bold, modern typeface.
* **Step 5:** Close Master View and verify the changes applied to all 5 slides instantly.

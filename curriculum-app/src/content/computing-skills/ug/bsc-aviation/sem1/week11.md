## Key Concepts
- **Presentation Fundamentals**: The basics of creating slides, choosing layouts, and adding text and images.
- **Design Principles**: Keeping slides uncluttered, highly readable, and visually appealing.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Slide Layout**: The arrangement of placeholders for text, images, and other objects on a slide.
- **Placeholder**: A pre-formatted container on a slide into which content is placed.

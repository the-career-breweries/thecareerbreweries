# Overview

Welcome to Session 4. Today we will be covering **Past and Future Tenses**.

---

# 1.4.1 Core Concept

* **Simple Past**: Completed actions at a specific time (e.g., 'The plane landed').
* **Past Continuous**: Interrupted past actions (e.g., 'I was inspecting the gear when...').
* **Future Tenses**: Schedules, plans, or predictions (e.g., 'We will depart shortly').

---

# 1.4.2 Application

* **Incident Reports**: Must maintain strict consistency in past tenses to accurately reflect timelines.
* **Passenger Announcements**: Rely heavily on clear future tenses to set expectations.
* **Shift Briefings**: Combining past review and future forecasting.

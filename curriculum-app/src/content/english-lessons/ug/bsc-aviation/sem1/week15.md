# Session 15: Scanning Techniques

---

# Overview

Welcome to Session 15. Today we will be covering **Scanning Techniques**.

---

# 3.3.1 Core Concept

* **Scanning**: Reading rapidly to find specific microscopic facts, dates, numbers, or keywords.
* **Visual Cues**: Using capital letters, numbers, bold text, and formatting to locate data.
* **Tracking**: Using a finger or pen to guide the eyes quickly down a page.

---

# 3.3.2 Application

* **Manifests**: Locating specific passenger names on a dense flight manifest.
* **Schedules**: Finding specific flight times, gate numbers, or equipment types on a timetable.
* **Manuals**: Finding a specific regulation number in a 500-page compliance manual.

---

# 3.3.3 Practice

* **Drill**: Scan a dense flight schedule timetable to find 5 specific connecting flights in under 2 minutes.
* **Search**: Locate 3 specific regulatory clauses in a sample document.
* **Race**: Compete to find specific data points in a text the fastest.

# Session 21: Professional Email Writing

---

# Overview

Welcome to Session 21. Today we will be covering **Professional Email Writing**.

---

# 4.3.1 Core Concept

* **BLUF**: Bottom Line Up Front - putting the most important information first.
* **Subject Lines**: Crafting clear, actionable, and specific subject lines (e.g., 'ACTION REQUIRED: Shift Swap').
* **Conciseness**: Using bullet points and short paragraphs to make emails skimmable.

---

# 4.3.2 Application

* **Inbox Management**: Ensuring your urgent requests are actually read and prioritized by busy managers.
* **Digital Etiquette**: Avoiding 'Reply All' disasters, using CC/BCC correctly, and maintaining a professional signature.
* **Efficiency**: Saving time for both the sender and the receiver.

---

# 4.3.3 Practice

* **Critique**: Analyze a poorly written, rambling email and identify its flaws.
* **Rewrite**: Create a strong, actionable subject line for 5 different email scenarios.
* **Draft**: Write a concise email to a manager summarizing a shift handover using BLUF.

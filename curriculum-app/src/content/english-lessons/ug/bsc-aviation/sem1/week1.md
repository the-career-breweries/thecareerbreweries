# Overview

Welcome to Session 1. Today we will be covering **Introduction to Nouns, Pronouns, and Adjectives**.

---

# 1.1.1 Core Concept

* **Nouns**: Identify people, places, things, or concepts (e.g., Captain, Tarmac, Safety).
* **Pronouns**: Replace nouns to avoid repetitive text (e.g., He, She, They, It).
* **Adjectives**: Modify and describe nouns to add specific detail (e.g., *Heavy* turbulence).

---

# 1.1.2 Application

* **Precision**: Use specific nouns (e.g., 'Boeing 737' instead of 'Plane') in professional logs.
* **Clarity**: Ensure pronouns clearly refer to the correct subject to prevent critical miscommunications.
* **Objectivity**: Avoid emotional adjectives in incident reports.

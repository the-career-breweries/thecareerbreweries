# Overview

Welcome to Session 23. Today we will be covering **Fundamentals of Report Writing**.

---

# 4.5.1 Core Concept

* **Purpose of Reports**: Creating permanent, objective, factual records of events or operations.
* **Structure**: Title, Executive Summary, Introduction, Findings/Body, Conclusion, Recommendations.
* **Objectivity**: Sticking purely to verifiable facts, removing all personal bias and emotion.

---

# 4.5.2 Application

* **Incident Reporting**: Accurately documenting safety incidents, accidents, or delays for legal and regulatory review.
* **Shift Reports**: Summarizing daily operations for the next shift to maintain continuity.
* **Decision Making**: Providing management with the clear data needed to make operational changes.

---

# 4.5.3 Practice

* **Outline**: Outline the required sections for a standard aviation incident report.
* **Fact-Check**: Strip the emotional and biased language out of a witness statement.
* **Draft**: Write a 3-sentence Executive Summary based on a page of raw operational data.

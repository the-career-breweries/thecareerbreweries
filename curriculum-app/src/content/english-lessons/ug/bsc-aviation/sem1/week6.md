# Overview

Welcome to Session 6. Today we will be covering **Sentence Types and Review**.

---

# 1.6.1 Core Concept

* **Simple Sentence**: One independent clause (e.g., 'The doors are closed').
* **Compound Sentence**: Two independent clauses joined by FANBOYS (e.g., 'The doors are closed, and we are ready').
* **Complex Sentence**: One independent + one dependent clause.

---

# 1.6.2 Application

* **Emergency**: Use simple sentences for emergency instructions where processing time is critical.
* **Analytical**: Use complex sentences for detailed analytical reports or policy documents.
* **Flow**: Mix sentence types to keep written correspondence engaging.

---

# 1.6.3 Practice

* **Combine**: Join pairs of simple sentences into compound sentences using correct conjunctions.
* **Identify**: Label the sentence types in a sample aviation manual.
* **Rewrite**: Simplify a complex paragraph into bulleted simple sentences.

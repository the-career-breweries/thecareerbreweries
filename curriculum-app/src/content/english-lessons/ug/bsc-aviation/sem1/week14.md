# Session 14: Skimming Techniques

---

# Overview

Welcome to Session 14. Today we will be covering **Skimming Techniques**.

---

# 3.2.1 Core Concept

* **Skimming**: Reading rapidly to get a general, macroscopic overview of the material.
* **Techniques**: Focusing on titles, headings, bullet points, first sentences of paragraphs, and conclusions.
* **Ignoring Fluff**: Training the brain to skip over adjectives and filler words.

---

# 3.2.2 Application

* **Volume Processing**: Processing large volumes of reports, emails, or daily industry news efficiently.
* **Triage**: Quickly skimming an inbox to determine which emails are urgent.
* **Preparation**: Skimming a document right before a meeting to grasp the agenda.

---

# 3.2.3 Practice

* **Drill**: Skim a 2-page industry report in 60 seconds.
* **Test**: Answer 3 general questions about the report's overarching theme.
* **Draft**: Write a 2-sentence 'gist' summary based only on skimming.

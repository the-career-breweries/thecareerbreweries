# Session 11: Technical Vocabulary - Passenger Handling

---

# Overview

Welcome to Session 11. Today we will be covering **Technical Vocabulary - Passenger Handling**.

---

# 2.5.1 Core Concept

* **Ticketing Jargon**: PNR, Itinerary, E-ticket, Codeshare, Overbooking.
* **Boarding terms**: Gate No-show, Standby, Final Call, Upgrade.
* **Special Categories**: UM (Unaccompanied Minor), PRM (Passenger with Reduced Mobility), VIP.

---

# 2.5.2 Application

* **Customer Service**: Clear communication with passengers regarding their ticketing and boarding status.
* **Coordination**: Using acronyms like 'UM' quickly communicates special needs to the cabin crew.
* **Efficiency**: Processing passengers at the gate requires rapid understanding of these terms.

---

# 2.5.3 Practice

* **Quiz**: Match the passenger handling acronym to its full form and definition.
* **Drafting**: Write a boarding announcement calling for PRMs and UMs first.
* **Role Play**: Handle a passenger asking about their standby status at the gate.

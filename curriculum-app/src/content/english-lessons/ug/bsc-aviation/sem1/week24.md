# Session 24: Report Writing Application & Review

---

# Overview

Welcome to Session 24. Today we will be covering **Report Writing Application & Review**.

---

# 4.6.1 Core Concept

* **Application**: Bringing paragraph structure, objective vocabulary, and formal tone together.
* **Peer Review**: Actively reviewing and critiquing colleagues' work to catch errors.
* **Proofreading**: Checking for grammar, tense consistency, spelling, and factual accuracy before submission.

---

# 4.6.2 Application

* **Quality Assurance**: Refining drafts to ensure 100% accuracy before submission to management or regulators.
* **Collaboration**: Working as a team to finalize complex operational documents.
* **Final Polish**: Ensuring the document reflects the highest level of professionalism.

---

# 4.6.3 Practice

* **Draft**: Write a complete operational shift report based on a provided raw data scenario.
* **Peer Review**: Swap reports with a partner and provide constructive feedback using a rubric.
* **Assess**: Final Formative Assessment on Writing Skills.

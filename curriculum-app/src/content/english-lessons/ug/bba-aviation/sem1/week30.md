# Overview

Welcome to Session 30. Today we will be covering **Role Play: Conflict Resolution & Review**.

---

# 5.6.1 Core Concept

* **Conflict Resolution**: De-escalating aggressive behavior and managing tension.
* **The HEART Model**: Hear, Empathize, Apologize, Resolve, Thank.
* **Inter-departmental Conflict**: Resolving clashes between ground staff and flight crew professionally.

---

# 5.6.2 Application

* **Safety & Security**: Preventing verbal conflicts from escalating into physical or security threats onboard.
* **Team Cohesion**: Maintaining a professional working environment despite disagreements with colleagues.
* **Resilience**: Managing personal stress and maintaining a calm exterior under pressure.

---

# 5.6.3 Practice

* **De-escalation Drill**: Practice using a calm, lowered voice and open body language against a shouting partner.
* **Role Play 1**: De-escalate an angry passenger demanding a free upgrade.
* **Role Play 2**: Resolve a miscommunication conflict between the gate agent and the cabin crew.

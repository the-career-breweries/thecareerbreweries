# Overview

Welcome to Session 25. Today we will be covering **Fundamentals of Active Listening**.

---

# 5.1.1 Core Concept

* **Hearing vs Listening**: Hearing is a passive biological function; listening is an active, psychological choice.
* **The SOFTEN Technique**: Smile, Open posture, Forward lean, Touch (appropriate), Eye contact, Nod.
* **Barriers**: Physical (noise), Psychological (bias), Semantic (jargon).

---

# 5.1.2 Application

* **Trust Building**: Active listening builds immediate rapport and trust with passengers.
* **Safety**: Catching critical details from colleagues during handovers prevents accidents.
* **Empathy**: Validating a speaker's feelings helps de-escalate tension.

---

# 5.1.3 Practice

* **Identify**: List 3 personal barriers to listening you experience and how to overcome them.
* **Pair Exercise**: One person speaks for 2 minutes, the other must listen silently and then paraphrase it back perfectly.
* **Body Language**: Practice the SOFTEN technique in a 1-on-1 interview setup.

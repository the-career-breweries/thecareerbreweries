# Session 4
## Communicative English

---

## Key Concepts
- **Skimming**: Reading rapidly in order to get a general overview of the material.
- **Scanning**: Reading rapidly in order to find specific facts or keywords.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Comprehension**: The action or capability of understanding something read or heard.
- **Inference**: A conclusion reached on the basis of evidence and reasoning from the text.


---

## Mnemonic: SQ3R
- S - Survey
- Q - Question
- R - Read
- R - Recite
- R - Review


---

## Knowledge Check (BTL)
- **Remember**: List the steps of the SQ3R method.
- **Understand**: Explain the difference between skimming and scanning.
- **Apply**: Skim a provided METAR report to find the general weather condition.
- **Analyze**: Identify the author's tone in a given passage.
- **Evaluate**: Determine the reliability of a given technical bulletin.
- **Create**: Formulate three comprehension questions for a text.

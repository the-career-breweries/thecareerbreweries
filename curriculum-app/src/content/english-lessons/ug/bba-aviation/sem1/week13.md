# Session 13
## Communicative English

---

## Key Concepts
- **Global Awareness**: Understanding and respecting cultural differences in communication styles, norms, and taboos.
- **Sensitivity & Adaptability**: Modifying one's communication approach to be inclusive and easily understood by diverse nationalities.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Ethnocentrism**: Evaluation of other cultures according to preconceptions originating in the standards and customs of one's own culture.
- **High-Context Culture**: Cultures that rely heavily on non-verbal and subtle situational cues in communication.


---

## Mnemonic: RICE
- R - Respect
- I - Inquire
- C - Communicate clearly
- E - Empathize


---

## Knowledge Check (BTL)
- **Remember**: Define a high-context culture.
- **Understand**: Explain why idiomatic expressions should be avoided in cross-cultural communication.
- **Apply**: Adapt a standard airline greeting for an international flight.
- **Analyze**: Analyze a scenario where a cultural misunderstanding led to a service failure.
- **Evaluate**: Assess your own cultural biases.
- **Create**: Design a brief cultural sensitivity guide for new cabin crew.

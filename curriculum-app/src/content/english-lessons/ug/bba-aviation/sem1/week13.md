# Overview

Welcome to Session 13. Today we will be covering **Introduction to Reading Comprehension**.

---

# 3.1.1 Core Concept

* **Active Reading**: Engaging with the text, asking questions, and taking notes, rather than passive scanning.
* **Main Idea vs Details**: Separating the central point from the supporting evidence.
* **The 5 Ws**: Identifying Who, What, Where, When, and Why in any document.

---

# 3.1.2 Application

* **Efficiency**: Quickly grasping the core message of company memos or policy updates saves time.
* **Briefings**: Extracting the main idea from a pre-flight briefing document to share with the team.
* **Prioritization**: Knowing what information is critical versus what is just background context.

---

# 3.1.3 Practice

* **Extract**: Read a sample airline memo and extract the 5 Ws.
* **Identify**: Highlight the main idea sentence in three different paragraphs.
* **Summarize**: Write a one-sentence summary of a page-long article.

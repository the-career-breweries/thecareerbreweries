# Session 11
## Communicative English

---

## Key Concepts
- **Structuring a Presentation**: Organizing content logically using an Introduction, Body, and Conclusion.
- **Visual Aids**: Using slides, charts, or props effectively without them overshadowing the speaker.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Hook**: An engaging opening statement designed to capture the audience's attention immediately.
- **Transition**: A word, phrase, or sentence that smoothly connects one topic or slide to the next.


---

## Mnemonic: PREP
- P - Point
- R - Reason
- E - Example
- P - Point (reiterate)


---

## Knowledge Check (BTL)
- **Remember**: List the three main sections of a presentation.
- **Understand**: Explain the rule of 6x6 for presentation slides.
- **Apply**: Draft a 'hook' for a presentation on airport security.
- **Analyze**: Compare a highly textual slide vs a highly visual slide.
- **Evaluate**: Assess the effectiveness of a peer's use of visual aids.
- **Create**: Design a 3-minute mini-presentation on a technical topic.

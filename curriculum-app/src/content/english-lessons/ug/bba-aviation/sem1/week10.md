# Overview

Welcome to Session 10. Today we will be covering **Technical Vocabulary - Flight Ops & ATC**.

---

# 2.4.1 Core Concept

* **Phonetic Alphabet**: Standardized words representing letters (Alpha, Bravo, Charlie) to ensure clarity over radio.
* **Standard Phraseology**: Strict ATC phrases (Roger, Wilco, Standby, Affirm, Negative).
* **Readbacks**: Repeating instructions to confirm correct understanding.

---

# 2.4.2 Application

* **Eliminating Errors**: Radio static and accents can cause fatal misunderstandings; phonetics eliminate this risk.
* **Global Standardization**: English is the international language of aviation; standard phraseology keeps it uniform.
* **Efficiency**: 'Wilco' replaces 'I have received your message and will comply'.

---

# 2.4.3 Practice

* **Drill**: Spell your full name and a sample flight number using the Phonetic Alphabet.
* **Translate**: Convert a layman's sentence into proper ATC phraseology.
* **Simulation**: Conduct a mock radio transmission requesting taxi clearance.

# Session 5
## Communicative English

---

## Key Concepts
- **Professional Formatting**: The standard layout and structure expected in business communications (emails, letters, reports).
- **Tone & Register**: The level of formality and attitude conveyed through word choice in writing.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Salutation**: A standard formula of words used in a letter to address the person being written to.
- **Sign-off**: The concluding phrase of an email or letter (e.g., Yours sincerely).


---

## Mnemonic: KISS
- K - Keep
- I - It
- S - Short &
- S - Simple


---

## Knowledge Check (BTL)
- **Remember**: State the standard components of a professional email.
- **Understand**: Explain when to use 'Yours faithfully' vs 'Yours sincerely'.
- **Apply**: Draft an email requesting sick leave.
- **Analyze**: Identify the tonal errors in a poorly written business letter.
- **Evaluate**: Critique a peer's incident report for clarity.
- **Create**: Write a formal incident report regarding a baggage delay.

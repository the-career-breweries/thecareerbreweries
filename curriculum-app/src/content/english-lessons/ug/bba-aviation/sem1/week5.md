# Overview

Welcome to Session 5. Today we will be covering **Sentence Structure Basics**.

---

# 1.5.1 Core Concept

* **SVO Structure**: Subject (Who/What) + Verb (Action) + Object (Receiver).
* **Clauses**: A group of words with a subject and verb (Independent vs Dependent).
* **Phrases**: A group of words missing either a subject or a verb.

---

# 1.5.2 Application

* **Standardization**: SVO prevents ambiguity in high-stress aviation environments.
* **Clarity**: Avoiding fragmented sentences ensures that instructions are legally and operationally sound.
* **Brevity**: Short, structured sentences are easier to transmit over radio.

---

# 1.5.3 Practice

* **Deconstruct**: Break down complex sentences into their core SVO components.
* **Fix**: Identify and correct fragmented or run-on sentences in a maintenance log.
* **Draft**: Write 5 perfect SVO sentences related to ground handling.

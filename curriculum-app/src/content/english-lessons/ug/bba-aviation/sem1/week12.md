# 2.6.1 Core Concept

* **Integration**: Smoothly combining general grammar rules with technical aviation terminology.
* **Acronym Management**: Knowing when to use an acronym and when to spell it out for the audience.
* **Review**: Assessing mastery of Prefixes, Synonyms, and Technical vocabulary.

# 2.6.2 Application

* **Audience Awareness**: Professional email drafting requires knowing not to over-rely on jargon when speaking to passengers.
* **Fluency**: Smoothly recalling technical terms without pausing during briefings.
* **Accuracy**: Ensuring the correct term is used to prevent operational delays.

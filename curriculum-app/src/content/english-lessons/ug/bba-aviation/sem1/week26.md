# Session 26: Phonetics and Pronunciation

---

# Overview

Welcome to Session 26. Today we will be covering **Phonetics and Pronunciation**.

---

# 5.2.1 Core Concept

* **Phonetics**: Mastering English vowel and consonant sounds to neutralize confusing accents.
* **Word Stress**: The emphasis given to a certain syllable (e.g., RE-cord vs re-CORD changes noun to verb).
* **Sentence Intonation**: The rise and fall of the voice (e.g., rising for questions, falling for statements).

---

# 5.2.2 Application

* **Clarity**: Preventing miscommunication over radio or PA systems where audio quality is poor.
* **International Ops**: Ensuring smooth communication with international colleagues or passengers with different native accents.
* **Professionalism**: Clear pronunciation projects confidence and authority.

---

# 5.2.3 Practice

* **Drill**: Practice tongue twisters to improve articulation and consonant clarity.
* **Marking**: Mark the correct word stress on a list of 20 common aviation terms.
* **Read Aloud**: Practice reading a PA announcement with correct intonation and stress.

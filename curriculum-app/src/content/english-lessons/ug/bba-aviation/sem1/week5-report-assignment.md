  <div style="flex: 1; padding: 1rem; border: 1px solid #ccc;">
    <h2>Report Writing Instructions</h2>
    <ol>
      <li><strong>Purpose</strong>: Summarise a factual incident, analyse causes, and propose recommendations.</li>
      <li><strong>Structure</strong> (use headings):
        <ul>
          <li>Title – clear and specific.</li>
          <li>Executive Summary – 2‑3 sentences (optional for longer reports).</li>
          <li>Introduction – context, purpose, and scope.</li>
          <li>Methodology – how information was gathered (e.g., crew statements, flight data).</li>
          <li>Findings – chronological description of the incident.</li>
          <li>Analysis – why it happened (weather, technical, human factors).</li>
          <li>Conclusion – concise statement of the main outcome.</li>
          <li>Recommendations – actionable steps to prevent recurrence.</li>
          <li>References – any sources (manuals, regulations, weather reports).</li>
        </ul>
      </li>
      <li><strong>Language</strong>: Use formal, objective tone. Prefer past tense for events, present simple for facts, and passive voice for actions when appropriate.</li>
      <li><strong>Style</strong>: Keep sentences concise (15‑20 words). Use transition words (therefore, consequently, however).</li>
      <li><strong>Formatting</strong>: 12‑pt Times New Roman or Arial, 1.5 line spacing, 1‑inch margins. Numbered headings (1., 1.1, 1.1.1). Include page numbers.</li>
      <li><strong>Word limit</strong>: Approximately 300‑350 words (≈1 page).</li>
    </ol>

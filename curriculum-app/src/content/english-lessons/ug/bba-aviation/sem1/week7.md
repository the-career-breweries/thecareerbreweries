## Communicative English

---

## Key Concepts
- **The 7 Cs of Communication**: Principles for effective communication: Clear, Concise, Concrete, Correct, Coherent, Complete, Courteous.
- **Barriers to Communication**: Obstacles (physical, psychological, semantic) that prevent accurate transmission of messages.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Semantic Barrier**: Misunderstanding arising from the different meanings that words can have.
- **Conciseness**: Giving a lot of information clearly and in a few words; brief but comprehensive.


---

## Mnemonic: 7 Cs
- C - Clear
- C - Concise
- C - Concrete
- C - Correct
- C - Coherent
- C - Complete
- C - Courteous


---

## Knowledge Check (BTL)
- **Remember**: List the 7 Cs of communication.
- **Understand**: Explain what constitutes a psychological barrier.
- **Apply**: Rewrite a wordy email to be concise.
- **Analyze**: Determine which of the 7 Cs is missing in a given vague message.
- **Evaluate**: Argue the importance of courtesy in crisis communication.
- **Create**: Develop a checklist for overcoming physical barriers in an airport.

# Overview

Welcome to Session 7. Today we will be covering **Introduction to Vocabulary Building**.

---

# 2.1.1 Core Concept

* **Active Vocabulary**: Words you understand and use frequently in speech and writing.
* **Passive Vocabulary**: Words you recognize when you read or hear them, but rarely use yourself.
* **Context Clues**: Guessing the meaning of an unknown word based on surrounding text.

---

# 2.1.2 Application

* **Professionalism**: Expanding active vocabulary enhances authority and clarity in business communications.
* **Adaptability**: Using context clues prevents panic when reading dense, unfamiliar regulatory documents.
* **Precision**: The right word saves time and prevents confusion.

---

# 2.1.3 Practice

* **Exercise 1**: Use a dictionary to find and define 5 new aviation terms.
* **Exercise 2**: Read a paragraph and deduce the meaning of 3 highlighted words using context clues.
* **Application**: Write sentences actively using your newly learned words.

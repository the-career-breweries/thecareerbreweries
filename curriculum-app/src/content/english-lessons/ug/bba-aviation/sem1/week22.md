# Session 22: Advanced Email Scenarios

---

# Overview

Welcome to Session 22. Today we will be covering **Advanced Email Scenarios**.

---

# 4.4.1 Core Concept

* **Delivering Bad News**: Being direct but empathetic, offering alternatives when possible.
* **Persuasive Writing**: Using logic, data, and benefits to convince stakeholders.
* **The Sandwich Method**: Delivering negative feedback between two positive statements.

---

# 4.4.2 Application

* **De-escalation**: Calming irate customers or vendors through written text where tone of voice is absent.
* **Leadership**: Persuading management to adopt a new process or approve a budget request.
* **Diplomacy**: Maintaining positive relationships even when denying a request.

---

# 4.4.3 Practice

* **Revise**: Rewrite an aggressive email draft into a polite, professional response.
* **Draft**: Write an email to a passenger explaining that their baggage has been temporarily lost, offering solutions.
* **Role Play (Written)**: Exchange persuasive emails with a partner negotiating a schedule change.

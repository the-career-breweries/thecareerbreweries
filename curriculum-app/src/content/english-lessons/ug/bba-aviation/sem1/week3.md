# Overview

Welcome to Session 3. Today we will be covering **Present Tenses**.

---

# 1.3.1 Core Concept

* **Simple Present**: Facts, habits, schedules (e.g., 'The flight departs at 10:00').
* **Present Continuous**: Actions happening right now (e.g., 'We are boarding').
* **Present Perfect**: Past actions with present relevance (e.g., 'The pilot has arrived').

---

# 1.3.2 Application

* **Daily Ops**: Routine daily logs rely heavily on the simple present tense.
* **Live Updates**: Live updates to ATC or ground crew use present continuous.
* **Handovers**: Shift handover reports often utilize present perfect to summarize completed prep work.

# Session 20: Formal and Informal Letter Writing

---

# Overview

Welcome to Session 20. Today we will be covering **Formal and Informal Letter Writing**.

---

# 4.2.1 Core Concept

* **Format**: Standard business block format (dates, addresses, salutations, sign-offs).
* **Formal vs Informal**: Differences in vocabulary, tone, and structure based on the recipient.
* **Salutations**: 'Dear Sir/Madam' vs 'Hi Team', and Sign-offs ('Sincerely', 'Best Regards').

---

# 4.2.2 Application

* **External Comms**: Drafting formal letters to stakeholders, regulatory bodies, or partners.
* **Internal Comms**: Writing semi-formal memos to colleagues or informal notes to team members.
* **Brand Image**: Every external letter represents the airline's professional brand.

---

# 4.2.3 Practice

* **Format Check**: Correct the layout errors in a sample business letter.
* **Draft (Formal)**: Write a formal letter requesting information about an aviation training seminar.
* **Draft (Informal)**: Write an informal memo inviting the team to a brief meeting.

# Session 17: Interpretation and Critical Reading

---

# Overview

Welcome to Session 17. Today we will be covering **Interpretation and Critical Reading**.

---

# 3.5.1 Core Concept

* **Fact vs Opinion**: Differentiating between objective reality and subjective belief or perspective.
* **Inferences**: Reaching a logical conclusion based on evidence and reasoning, reading 'between the lines'.
* **Tone & Intent**: Identifying the author's attitude (e.g., urgent, formal, frustrated).

---

# 3.5.2 Application

* **Complaint Resolution**: Evaluating passenger complaint letters objectively, separating emotional opinions from factual service failures.
* **Incident Reports**: Identifying subjective bias in witness statements during an investigation.
* **Critical Thinking**: Not taking all industry news or reports at face value without evaluating the evidence.

---

# 3.5.3 Practice

* **Highlight**: Mark facts in green and opinions in red in a sample editorial article.
* **Infer**: Read a polite but firm email from management and infer the underlying urgency.
* **Discuss**: Compare two incident reports and identify which is more objective.

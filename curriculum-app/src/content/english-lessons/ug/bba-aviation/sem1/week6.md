# Session 6
## Communicative English

---

## Key Concepts
- **Pronunciation & Enunciation**: The correct articulation of words to ensure clarity, especially over radio or PA systems.
- **Active Listening**: Fully concentrating, understanding, responding, and remembering what is being said.


---

## Important Definitions
- **Articulation**: The formation of clear and distinct sounds in speech.
- **Intonation**: The rise and fall of the voice in speaking, conveying emotion or grammatical structure.


---

## Mnemonic: HEAR
- H - Hear the message
- E - Empathize
- A - Analyze
- R - Respond


---

## Knowledge Check (BTL)
- **Remember**: Define active listening.
- **Understand**: Explain how intonation changes the meaning of a sentence.
- **Apply**: Demonstrate a PA announcement with clear articulation.
- **Analyze**: Identify barriers to effective listening in a noisy environment.
- **Evaluate**: Assess a role-play partner's clarity and pronunciation.
- **Create**: Design a role-play script for handling a passenger complaint.
